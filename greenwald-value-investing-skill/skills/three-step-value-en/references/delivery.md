# Host research, review, and delivery

## Light completion records

For each method preserve `input.json`, `valuation.json`, actual calculation, and `report.md`. The host may use a light `stages.json` for materials/plan/research/synthesis/calculation/review/final with pending/completed/skipped and a short explanation; no required format. The host can research directly using available material. Record native-agent answers only when agents were actually dispatched and external-model calls only when actually made. Do not fabricate calls directories, token accounts, proofs, or certificates.

Plan around decisive questions. Focused research supplies actual facts, estimates, derivations, and counterevidence. Synthesize reasons for adopting/modifying/rejecting findings without averaging incompatible conclusions. After initial Python calculation, review economic scope, material omissions, currency, units, arithmetic, and double counting. Change final parameters and actually recalculate. Record tool failures and continue with available sources/reasonable assumptions; small gaps do not mandate extra rounds. Growth investigate addresses one applicability question once before convergence.

Where the host permits native subagents, select bounded independent tasks by complexity: current facts, funds with matching obligations, maintenance, or a decisive assumption. Supply relevant original material and scope without prescribing expected conclusions or asking every agent to redo all methods. The lead owns adoption, deduplication, scope consistency, and final calculation. No required agent count, rounds, or paid CLI; work directly without native agents.

Economic review returns to critical source text to confirm signs, units, and totals/subtotals, rather than only recomputing entered numbers. Distinguish disclosed facts from normal assumptions. Explain why an estimated normal burden differs from the actual reporting-period amount.

Concentrate substantive review on the few assumptions that could change the conclusion: do they acknowledge latest relevant facts, make economic sense, and match the final parameters? Relevant examples include restricted-resource release and obligations, maintenance basis, or group growth mapping and investment. Correct observed inconsistencies and recalculate; “conservative” or merely mentioning an issue is not verification. Do not turn this into universal item gates or mandatory re-research.

If interrupted, preserve completed results and unperformed stages. Preparation success is not completed research. Recalculating old work does not create fresh reading/review or turn skip/defer into evaluate. Without Python execution, deliver parameters/research with “calculation not executed”; mental arithmetic or JSON output is not program verification.

## Report reading order

Write the final host-authored report in plain English. Open with a brief conclusion, decisive reasons, and constraints. Use readable millions/billions with currency; retain precision in the ledger. The unchanged shared calculator produces a technical `report.md` that can contain Chinese headings, warnings, and generated explanations. Preserve it and use saved calculation numbers to produce the English narrative; do not imply a fully localized calculator or edit the technical report's totals.

- Assets: premise; operating/nonoperating resources; liabilities; NCI/preferred; parent-common and per-share value. Distinguish new replacement cost from current net resources.
- EPV: normal EBIT → distributable earnings; core EPV → parent-common equity. Explain actual maintenance, tax, and capital-cost bases.
- Growth evaluate: advantage and growth scope; Q/B/M; D/I/A funding; three return components; decay; capital-cost gap; valid scenarios. D/M is not personal dividend yield.
- Growth skip/defer: applicability, main evidence/counterevidence, references to assets/EPV, and upstream implications without empty numeric tables.

Separate disclosures/observed data, industry conventions, independent estimates, user policies, and program outputs. List actual reports/pages/sections and URLs/files. Distinguish full documents prepared from sections read and disclose tool/material gaps. Do not list unread sections as coverage. Preserve conflicts, major upstream unknowns, and counterevidence. “Conservative” cannot justify known double deductions.

Distinguish valuation date, research/evidence cutoff, equity-bridge stock date, price date, and FX date. Mark missing dates as unspecified; do not infer updates from a cutoff. Actually compare at least two full years; EPV additionally explains adopted longer history and cycle limits. Unverified price/FX sources are not certified quotes.

The methods can jointly explain resource replication, normal earnings, and new investment while retaining distinct results. Without aligned dates, scope, currency, leases, and completeness, do not state exact differences. Do not add assets/EPV or turn growth returns into a total growth price. Asset value is not guaranteed liquidation proceeds; EPV is not a price floor; conditional return is not actual IRR. Distinguish arithmetic completion, research completion, applicability, user-return requirements, and a purchase decision.

Show decisive estimates through scenarios without averaging incompatible cases or calling sensitivity ranges probability confidence intervals. Distinguish unknown values from economic nonexistence. Label unquantified decay results pre-decay. Qualitative analysis can support a reasoned conditional judgment, but not a claim that risk-adjusted net returns were verified.

When the user requests all three methods, write a separate English `combined_summary.md` as the primary reading deliverable, synthesized from saved final calculations and method conclusions: resources, normal earnings value, growth applicability/returns at this price, key disagreements, and shared risks. Link each independent technical report and ledger; never calculate another combined value. A single-method request does not require this summary.
