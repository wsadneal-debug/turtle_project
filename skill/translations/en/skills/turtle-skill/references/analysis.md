# Analysis: from facts to the group and shareholders
For steps 1–6; data acquisition, price formulas and installation are documented elsewhere.

## Five years, latest quarters and seasonality
Aim for five comparable years of revenue, attributable profit, operating cash, capital expenditure, FCF, share count and per-share distributions; extend to cycle troughs where needed. Continue with shorter coverage while stating gaps; never fabricate years or call short history cross-cycle evidence.

After matching entity, currency, scale, accounting definition and consolidation scope, split cumulative period flows only:
```text
Q2 = H1 - Q1
Q3 = 9M - H1
Q4 = FY - 9M
H2 = FY - H1
QoQ_pct = (Q_latest / Q_previous - 1) * 100
YoY_pct = (Q_latest / Q_same_quarter_prior_year - 1) * 100
HoH_pct = (H_latest / H_previous - 1) * 100
```
Use the preceding year's Q4/H2 across year boundaries and actual start/end dates for non-calendar fiscal years. Use percentages only with positive, interpretable bases; for zero, negative or near-zero bases show original values, absolute changes and loss/profit transitions. Do not split balance-sheet cash, debt, receivables or inventory this way.

Both sequential comparisons require industry seasonality, holidays, weather, promotions and delivery/acceptance timing, checked against two or three years of comparable seasons and peers. Large normal seasonal changes are allowed, but seasonality must not become a blanket excuse. Distinguish ordinary deceleration, material stalling, absolute contraction and structural decline; narrowing losses are not renewed growth, and positive H1 growth cannot conceal a Q2 reversal.

When programs cannot reliably supply periods, extract formal disclosures and label model calculations. Missing Q1 does not permit H1/2 as invented Q2. Quarterly retail turnover can be a proxy signal, not mixed with recognized group revenue; a change in YoY growth is not amount-based QoQ. Channel-loading/destocking explanations also need sell-in, receivables/rebates, channel inventory, sell-through and subsequent replenishment.

## Project event-driven revenue, then scale
For events capable of changing the conclusion, identify the baseline, affected business, price/volume mechanism, realization conditions, magnitude and timing. Plans, capacity and orders each have realization conditions; none equals realized revenue/cash. Prefer supported ranges or bounds; missing precision is not a reason to stop reasoning or invent probabilities.

```text
BusinessRevenue = RealizedVolume * RealizedPrice
DeltaGroupRevenue = Revenue_with_event - Revenue_without_event
GroupRevenueImpact_pct = AffectedRevenueShare * BusinessRevenueChange_pct
```
In the last expression the share is a fraction and the change is a percentage number; it estimates direct effects only with matching baseline periods/scopes and other businesses unchanged. Do not apply realization factors twice. Estimate new-business incremental revenue against group scale instead of a zero-base growth rate. Avoid double counting overlapping events and cannibalization; local high growth is not group high growth.

Costs, taxes, capital expenditure, disposals, financing and share-count events may affect profit, cash or per-share outcomes directly, without revenue first. Use the relevant revenue/profit/cash/asset denominator. Do not miss group-wide license, key-customer or cross-default transmission because direct revenue weight is small. End at recurring profit, working capital, necessary capital expenditure, shares, per-share cash and dividends, not revenue growth mechanically mapped to profit growth.

## Six dimensions and capital allocation
| Dimension | Decisive question |
|---|---|
| Business economics | Why do customers pay, and what capital/working capital sustains profit? |
| Competitive position | Can retention, costs, pricing or share advantages persist against comparable peers? |
| Operating trajectory | What are the true long-term and latest-quarter direction, drivers and persistence? |
| Cash quality | How does profit convert to operating cash and cash after necessary investment? |
| Financial resilience | What funds are usable after restrictions, ownership, debt maturities and known spending? |
| Capital discipline | What per-share outcomes result from investment, holding cash, further capital, after-tax exits and distributions? |

Strong net cash does not establish good conversion; investigate aging, collection and timing behind receivables/inventory/payables, not balances alone. Lost earnings after a successful exit first change the operating base, not negate full-cycle success. Undeployed funds or missing IRR are not realized destruction. Mixed capital discipline needs materially comparable real positive and negative outcomes; examine inefficient expansion, dilution, related arrangements and delivery. Formally established serious integrity failures, still attributable to the current organization without substantive isolation, cap quality at C; ordinary penalties follow economic effects, and rumors are not findings.

A+ denotes a rare durable combination of economics, competition and shareholder outcomes; A is clearly high quality, B viable but ordinary/mixed, C materially weak, D fundamentally impaired business/shareholder interests, and unresolved direction remains unrated. Integrate importance rather than voting, averaging, price or evidence counts.

## Industry adaptation, countercase and conclusion
Match peers by business, price tier, customers, geography, channels and capital model; analyze conglomerates by profit pool. Cyclicals need trough/maintenance-capital analysis; banks need asset quality/regulatory capital, insurers underwriting/reserves/capital, and property development differs from rental. Do not force generic FCF onto these. Software needs retention, capitalization and equity dilution; holding companies upstream rights; early-stage firms bounded evidence-based business analysis.

Test the strongest alternative explanation. Inherit historical matters but establish whether resolved, superseded or still active; an old rating is not new evidence. Separately assess stable non-deteriorating fundamentals and operational/financial/delivery support for dividends. Material unknowns do not pass, but missing evidence does not automatically mean poor quality. Confirmed non-seasonal stalling/decline must feed annual cash and distribution scenarios into both price references; cheap downside research values cannot waive the business prerequisite.
