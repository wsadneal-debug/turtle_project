# Value, investor receipts and five decisions

## Three separate questions
**Original strategy eligibility:** read [cash payback](absolute-valuation.md), [returns](lookthrough-return.md) and [headroom](headroom.md) and recalculate verifiable inputs. Six-year attributable enterprise cash coverage compares net cash plus six years of FCF with market cap. It does not deduct investor dividend tax, add a sale price or require six years of dividends to repay the purchase. Incomplete inputs support only the relevant reference/unknown result. The 65% research threshold is not a buy threshold.

**Independent value:** before quotes, 07A selects one simple method suited to economic substance. State normalized cash/earnings, capital cost or multiple, maintenance spending, debt, attribution, terminal treatment and assumption sources. Keep enterprise and equity bases consistent; enterprise cash value needs an equity bridge, whereas equity cash must not have debt deducted twice. Cash capitalization/total-capex proxies may be references but are not strict EPV without maintenance/funding bridges. Required operating assets' replacement cost is not distributable cash; avoid adding net cash or disposal proceeds twice. Supported ranges and sensitivities suffice without a precise target. Do not force general FCF methods onto unsuitable industries.

**Holder return:** list dated receipts and payments using reference purchase price, share count, buy/sell fees, currency/FX assumptions, tax, dividend entitlement/payment dates and an explicit terminal-sale assumption. Initial investment is negative; dividends and sale proceeds follow actual or assumed dates. Company buybacks are not direct cash paid to the investor. Tax gross dividends once, never tax net dividends twice or apply dividend tax to company FCF, buybacks or terminal capital. Dividends with established rights acquired before ex-date remain on their actual payment date even after sale; buying on/after ex-date or selling before it does not imply entitlement. Verify special-market entitlement rules.

Use a tested host XIRR/numerical solver only with a complete dated schedule. Multiple sign changes, absent roots or multiple roots require disclosure, not an invented unique return. A complete after-cost return needs adequate amounts, dates, fees and tax assumptions. Missing fees may support explicit zero-fee sensitivity, not a silently complete calculation. The bundled calculator provides basic dual-price arithmetic, not XIRR or full independent valuation; never claim those operations ran.

## Five decisions
| Item | Choices |
|---|---|
| Material-loss risk | Low / medium / high / unresolved |
| Company valuation | Clearly undervalued / moderately undervalued / fair / expensive / unresolved |
| Broad-market valuation | Same choices, independent of the company |
| Industry valuation | Same choices, with index/peer-sample scope |
| Action | Enter human buy-decision process / wait for a lower price / wait for decisive facts / reject this transaction |

Support each with decisive evidence and group magnitude. Missing personal funding/position information limits suitability, not company research. Incomplete market/industry history does not automatically reject the company. A single stock's low PE does not establish a cheap industry; identify loss-making/abnormal-earnings distortions. When waiting, specify obtainable evidence or a supported estimate that could resolve the issue, not guarantees about every future event.

Independent value and holder return can disagree with original strategy eligibility; preserve disagreement and reconcile economic bases. A low price does not offset material operating decline or integrity problems. Supported qualitative judgments are allowed without numerical verification, while original price conditions remain explicitly unconfirmed. Research adjudication is not a universal investment instruction and does not execute trades.
