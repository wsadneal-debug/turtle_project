# Due-diligence workflow (portable V2.3 adaptation)

## Inputs and working papers
After explicit opt-in, establish company/ticker, cutoff and evidence scope. Usable official disclosures or attachments suffice; proprietary JSON, database snapshots or a system report are not prerequisites. Existing research is a lead: recheck load-bearing originals and calculations. Retrieve public originals or limit work to attachments; “the report says” is not an accounting fact.

If file tools exist, use a new task directory for material references, per-question findings, calculation inputs/results, challenges, scenarios and the report. Otherwise retain the same information in conversation sections. Record source, publication date, reporting period, currency, units and the actual location read. Separate facts, arithmetic, estimates and unknowns, citing real evidence. Do not require evidence quotas, content hashes or completeness certificates.

## Sequence and quote visibility
- Plan: identify conclusion-changing questions without prematurely recommending a purchase.
- Before quotes: 01–06 investigate operations, cash, capital and governance; 07A independently chooses an appropriate valuation method and supported assumptions. Keep quotes, broker targets and old ratings apart from this stage.
- After quotes: 07B compares the price with 07A; 08 assesses market/industry context; 09 considers transaction terms and holder receipts; 10 actively tests material-loss paths.
- Synthesize: resolve disagreement by mechanism and group magnitude, not votes. Challenge only remaining issues that could alter the result; proceed to scenarios if none remain.
- Challenge: pass complete decisive findings and opposing evidence to the relevant direction. Resolve the specific gap and remaining effects without repeating the whole company study. Withdraw counterarguments weakened by evidence.
- Scenarios: define baseline, adverse and severe mechanisms, conditions and evidence, each with its own cash assumptions and actual arithmetic. Qualitative cases are valid; no default loss percentages. A severe case is not a probability forecast and worst cases need not all coexist.
- Adjudicate: separate the five decisions and three valuation/return concepts, explain material choices and the primary reversal condition, and recalculate revised assumptions.

These are research steps, not mandatory seats or extra calls. One model may execute them sequentially, but cannot erase quote exposure by being told to ignore it. Prior quotes, search results revealing market cap/targets or reused priced reports must be disclosed as a failure of strict price blindness, not sanitized into independence. Actually isolate inputs only with an authorized independent context. Inherit base-strategy interpretations only from the same provider, method 5.2.2 or later, and the latest concise result within the preceding 365 days. Other models' answers are attributed opinions to recheck, not one's own independent verification. If multiple models are authorized for this separate due-diligence exercise, preserve their provenance and disagreement without voting or relabeling the combination as a single-provider base run.

## Budget and stopping
Do not inherit internal production budgets of dozens/hundreds of calls. Agree a scope within user limits and host capabilities; without authorization for added services, use the current conversation. Share verifiable originals and calculations across directions, investigating only decisive gaps. Task count is not quality.

At a budget/time/host stop or with no feasible useful evidence left, preserve a partial report instead of restarting automatically. Resume only on explicit user request, reusing verified same-task material/calculations and retaining prior consumption. Do not reset budgets or switch providers to continue. Supplemental work reruns only affected directions and downstream judgments and identifies inherited work. Without background capability, do not invent watchers/services or repeatedly wake models to inspect status.

## Report
Lead with an investment judgment and reasons, then separate the operating/dividend base case, five decisions, original strategy conditions/independent value/holder return, material counterarguments and challenge resolutions, three scenarios, and one primary reversal condition with obtainable decisive evidence. Append per-question conclusions, sources and major calculations. A separate technical note records the actual model, scope, quote exposure, unexecuted steps and stopping reason. Ordinary missing details do not mechanically disqualify the business; limited evidence is not a comprehensive audit.
